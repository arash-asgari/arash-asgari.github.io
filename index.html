What I work on

Research

My research combines analytical modelling, computational methods, and data-driven validation to address operational challenges in service systems.

01

Stochastic service systems

I develop queueing models for systems where service capacity changes over time and customers may abandon while waiting. This work includes model formulation, parameter estimation, computational methods, and diffusion approximations.

Queueing theory · Staffing · Service capacity

02

Healthcare operations

I use mathematical modelling and simulation to study resource allocation and patient flow in healthcare settings, including exam-room assignments in surgical clinics and admission decisions for clinical decision units.

Patient flow · Resource allocation · Simulation

03

Pricing and control of service networks

I study how dynamic pricing and scheduling decisions can improve the operation of service networks, including battery-swapping networks for sustainable electric mobility.

Dynamic pricing · Scheduling · Sustainable mobility
